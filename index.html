A personal assistant for one account.

WhiteNova Hermes is a privately operated personal productivity assistant. It helps its sole user work with email, calendars, contacts, files, documents and spreadsheets through Google APIs.

Not a public service. The application is not offered to other users and does not sell, advertise or distribute user data.
