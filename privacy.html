W WhiteNova Hermes Privacy

PRIVACY POLICY

Plain language. No hidden data circus.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

1. Scope

WhiteNova Hermes is a private, non-commercial application operated solely for its owner. It is not offered as a service to the public.

2. Google data accessed

With the user's explicit authorization, the application may access Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts. Access can include reading and organizing email, sending user-directed email, reading or managing calendar events, and working with files, documents, spreadsheets and contact information.

3. How data is used

Google user data is used only to provide personal-assistant functions requested or configured by the authorized user. It is not used for advertising, profiling, credit decisions or purposes unrelated to those functions.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with unrelated third parties. Data may be processed by infrastructure and service providers only where technically necessary to perform a user-requested function and subject to their applicable security and privacy terms.

5. Storage and retention

OAuth credentials are stored on privately controlled infrastructure and retained until access is revoked or the integration is removed. Google content is processed only as needed for requested functions. User-directed notes, summaries or records may be retained in the owner's private knowledge system until the owner deletes them.

6. Security

Access is restricted to the application owner. Connections to Google APIs use encrypted HTTPS transport. Credentials are not intentionally exposed in public source code or public web pages.

7. Google API Limited Use

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. Control and revocation

The user can revoke the application's access at any time from the third-party access section of their Google Account. Revocation prevents further access and the locally stored OAuth credentials can then be removed.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the developer contact address displayed on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
